## Decision

Use Inve as a research shortcut for its publicly accessible pages, beginning with the six target-ledger examples. Verify any claim used in FERE against an archived original concall/filing and an official later NSE/BSE result, recording date, scope, units, pages, URLs and hashes. Do not copy grades or bulk ingest vendor text without access/licence approval. In the local portfolio database ManagementClaims has zero rows and the new verified evidence store has zero documents for held ISINs; therefore no local walk-the-talk verdict is currently publishable. Exclude funds, overseas securities and ambiguous unit-linked records from this Indian-company score.

BEL source-level spot check: the [issuer's 20 May 2025 transcript, p.21](https://bel-india.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Transcript-of-the-Conference-Call-held-on-20.05.2025.pdf) gives FY26 "revenue growth around 15%" guidance. [Inve's BEL page](https://inve.money/stock/BEL) reports 16.16% achieved. However, the [NSE FY26 consolidated filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ixbrl/INTEGRATED_FILING_INDAS_158847_19052026163923_iXBRL_WEB.html) reports FY26 revenue of ₹27,610.11 crore, whereas [BEL's provisional issuer release](https://bel-india.in/news-bel/bel-achieves-record-turnover-of-rs-26750-crores/) reports approximately ₹26,750 crore turnover versus ₹23,024 crore prior year (16.2%). These are not the same definition. The transcript does not specify consolidated revenue versus standalone turnover in that sentence; **do not score this claim until the intended basis and comparable audited FY25/FY26 values are pinned**. This pilot shows why a vendor verdict cannot be copied blindly.

Other discovered providers: [ConCallIQ](https://concalliq.in/rankings/management-credibility) has a public ranking shell but its records did not render in the read-only page; [QuarterMark](https://quartermark.in/guidance-scorecard) currently exposes a small verified example set; neither has yet been measured for all 69 names. [Inve methodology/product](https://inve.money/promise-tracker) states that full target histories are a paid feature.
