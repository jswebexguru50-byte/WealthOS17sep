## 1. Executive Summary & Consensus

The strongest verified operating trajectory in this six-company set is **LUMAXTECH**: FY2026 revenue grew 33.9%, PAT grew 47.1%, EBITDA margin held at roughly 14.2%, and CFO/PAT was 1.36x. Its main weakness is valuation: the stored 2026-10-02 secondary snapshot shows 43.68x P/E, while the primary-filing dataset does not contain enough balance-sheet and valuation history for a robust margin-of-safety conclusion.

**SONACOMS** has the strongest absolute margin profile among the industrial names with a verified FY2026 EBITDA margin of 25.8% and CFO/PAT of 1.05x. However, only one complete annual period is promoted in the current FERE card, and the stored secondary valuation is demanding at 74.44x P/E and 8.4x P/B. It is therefore a high-quality-looking but high-expectation case whose trend and valuation claims remain only partially evidenced.

**PROTEAN** delivered good FY2026 growth and modest margin expansion, but cash conversion weakened sharply and June-quarter PAT fell to only ₹5.86 crore. **JUSTDIAL** remains cash generative and apparently debt-light in the stored secondary snapshot, but FY2026 PAT fell 14.9% and EBITDA margin compressed by about 8 percentage points. **DBOL** grew from a low profit base, but its ~5.2% EBITDA margin is structurally thin and the June-quarter extraction contains a PBT/PAT inconsistency that prevents reliance on that quarter. **TARSONS** has a high reported FY2026 EBITDA margin but very weak PAT conversion and a June-quarter loss, making it the clearest current deterioration case.

## 2. Company Dossiers

### PROTEAN — Protean eGov Technologies

**Fundamental snapshot.** Consolidated FY2026 revenue was ₹997.75 crore, up 18.7% from ₹840.70 crore. EBITDA rose to ₹183.67 crore from ₹148.59 crore and margin expanded from 17.7% to 18.4%. PAT increased 8.8% to ₹100.59 crore, but CFO fell from ₹192.71 crore to ₹78.47 crore, reducing CFO/PAT from 2.08x to 0.78x. The divergence between earnings and operating cash flow is the main FY2026 quality concern. Q1 FY2027 PAT was only ₹5.86 crore; the local XBRL extraction does not expose a sufficiently complete Q1 revenue/EBITDA set, so no quarterly margin or YoY comparison is asserted.

**QGLP deep-dive.** Quality is mixed-positive: margin expanded and cash flow remained positive, but conversion weakened sharply. Growth is positive on FY2026 revenue and PAT. Longevity cannot be assessed from only two promoted annual observations and no verified business-driver history. Price cannot be assessed because current P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, market cap and historical valuation bands are absent from eligible local facts.

**Ownership / smart money.** The official June 2026 shareholding filing reports 0% promoter and 100% public ownership. This should be interpreted as an institutionally/publicly owned structure, not as a missing promoter value. FII/DII splits and ownership trend are unavailable. Promoter pledge is not applicable in the usual sense when promoter holding is zero, but the source field itself is null.

**Management walk-the-talk.** `NOT_VERIFIABLE`: no accepted management commitments or authenticated management-claim evidence are stored.

**Key risks and what to watch.** Watch CFO/PAT recovery, the cause of the June-quarter PAT contraction, and whether FY2026 growth translates into subsequent cash earnings. Debt, cash, receivables, inventory and working-capital data are required before judging balance-sheet resilience.

**Assessment.** `MIXED / MONITOR`. The FY2026 operating result is respectable, but the cash-flow deterioration and weak latest-quarter PAT prevent a supportive high-conviction conclusion.

### JUSTDIAL — Just Dial

**Fundamental snapshot.** Standalone FY2026 revenue was ₹1,213.86 crore, up 6.3%, while EBITDA declined to ₹670.30 crore from ₹721.92 crore. EBITDA margin compressed from 63.2% to 55.2%. PAT fell 14.9% to ₹497.02 crore and CFO declined to ₹268.70 crore from ₹311.49 crore; CFO/PAT was 0.54x. Q1 FY2027 PAT was ₹166.22 crore, but incomplete stored Q1 revenue/EBITDA fields prevent a like-for-like margin assessment.

**QGLP deep-dive.** Quality is challenged by simultaneous margin, PAT and CFO deterioration, though absolute profitability remains high. Growth is weak: top-line growth did not convert into earnings growth. Longevity and competitive position are not evidenced by the current stored filings. Price is superficially inexpensive at a stored 11.25x P/E, but this is a secondary snapshot and the primary dataset lacks cash, investments, enterprise value and normalized earnings detail. Low P/E alone is not sufficient to call it undervalued.

**Ownership / smart money.** Official promoter holding was 74.14% at 2026-06-30. The FII/DII split, QoQ ownership movement, bulk/block deals and promoter pledge value are not available in eligible official records. A stored secondary fact reports debt/equity of 0.0x, but this is not independently reproduced from balance-sheet XBRL in this review.

**Management walk-the-talk.** `NOT_VERIFIABLE`: no stored accepted commitments or evidence-backed target/actual comparisons.

**Key risks and what to watch.** Determine whether margin compression is structural or temporary, reconcile reported PAT with cash conversion, and obtain cash/investment balances before treating the low P/E as a valuation opportunity.

**Assessment.** `MIXED / VALUE WATCH`. The multiple is low relative to this cohort, but the verified direction of margins and earnings is negative.

### DBOL — Dhampur Bio Organics

That ratio requires working-capital and non-cash reconciliation before it can be treated as a quality signal.

**QGLP deep-dive.** Quality is uncertain because margins are thin and the cash-to-profit relationship is extreme. Growth is positive but profit growth is amplified by the low base. Longevity cannot be established without a longer normalized cycle. Price cannot be assessed: eligible local P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA and market-cap facts are absent.

**Ownership / smart money.** Official promoter holding was 50.78%, public holding 48.20%, and employee trusts 1.02% at 2026-06-30. Promoter pledge, FII/DII split and trend are missing.

**Management walk-the-talk.** `NOT_VERIFIABLE`: no authenticated management commitments are stored.

**Key risks and what to watch.** The June 2026 XBRL extraction reports PAT of ₹36.79 crore but PBT of negative ₹18.29 crore for the same consolidated period. Without a verified exceptional/tax bridge this is internally inconsistent, so the quarter is quarantined from trend analysis. Thin margins, finance costs, commodity/cycle sensitivity, debt and working-capital funding are the priority diligence items.

**Assessment.** `CAUTIOUS / DATA-QUALITY HOLD`. FY2026 improved, but the economics remain low-margin and the latest-quarter anomaly must be resolved before upgrading the view.

**Management walk-the-talk.** `NOT_VERIFIABLE`: no accepted target/actual commitments are stored.

**Key risks and what to watch.** The premium multiple creates de-rating risk if growth slows. Obtain debt, cash, receivables, inventory, capex and customer-concentration evidence; monitor whether CFO remains above PAT as the business scales.

**Assessment.** `POSITIVE FUNDAMENTALS / VALUATION WATCH`. This is the strongest verified operating result in the cohort, but not a demonstrated bargain.

## 6. Missing Information and Resolution Priority

### Common P0 gaps preventing full fundamental conviction

1. Verified balance sheets: gross debt, cash and investments, receivables, inventory, payables, net working capital and capital employed.
2. Complete quarterly P&L for at least eight quarters, with revenue, EBITDA/operating profit, PAT and comparable scope.
3. Cash-flow detail: capex, acquisitions, leases, free cash flow and working-capital movement.
4. Official multi-quarter shareholding: promoter, pledge, FII and DII changes.
5. Valuation inputs aligned to a dated price: shares outstanding, TTM EPS, enterprise value, P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield and historical/peer bands.
6. Authenticated annual-report / investor-presentation evidence for business mix, competitive position, customer concentration, segment margins, capacity and order visibility.
7. Management guidance and subsequent actuals for walk-the-talk assessment.
