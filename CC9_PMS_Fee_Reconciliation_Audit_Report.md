1. **Regulatory Ceiling vs. Contractual Rate**:
   * Previously, the line `"Operating Expenses Cap Test (0.50% p.a.)"` only tested whether total expenses exceeded the **SEBI statutory upper limit of 0.50% p.a.** (which is ~₹7.7 Lakhs). Because ₹1.65 Lakhs was below that statutory ceiling, the compliance test showed `₹0` regulatory violation.
2. **Actual Contractual Overcharge**:
   * When measured against your **actual contractual rates** in Schedule 2 (Custody @ 0.02% + FA @ 0.025% + GST):
     * In the first 4 quarters (Oct 2023 to Sep 2024), Complete Circle debited **₹98,255.45** as arbitrary lump-sum "Operating Expenses" (₹22.2k, ₹26.8k, ₹29.1k, ₹20.1k) without providing itemized invoices or custodian bills.
     * Combined across all operating/ancillary expenses, you were debited **₹1,65,256.43** vs. **₹85,299.77** expected under Schedule 2 rates, resulting in a **₹79,956.66 net overcharge on other charges**.
