# Parallel opportunity research constitution — proposed

Owner: Gopal Sharma. Status: draft for approval; this document governs the proposed parallel scan once adopted. It does not override applicable repository instructions or the existing forensic evidence constitution. It does not authorize changing scanner rules, thresholds or weights.

## Purpose

Start with the owner's existing 1,521-name universe and technical strategies, then help the owner investigate financially solid companies and executable opportunities. This is a research and decision-support system. The owner makes capital-allocation decisions.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Preserve scanner signals: no fundamental score, liquidity, pattern, breadth or review status silently adds, removes or changes scanner membership. Show counts and every source signal. User-selected optional filters are visible and reversible.
2. No strategy rule, indicator, threshold, weighting, price basis or entry/stop/target convention changes without explicit owner approval. Proposed alternatives run as labeled experiments only following approval. Preserve baseline and input snapshots.
3. Keep fundamental, technical, valuation, liquidity and data confidence separate. No combined score or calibrated probability until expressly approved and validated. No BUY, conviction or investment-grade verdict inferred from unvalidated signals.
4. Preserve facts, times and uncertainty: source, retrieved time, reporting period, basis, publication time when known, identity and units accompany observations. Distinguish unknown, not applicable, failed fetch, stale and verified absence. Missing evidence is never a positive result.
5. Event time is not knowledge time. Historical signal calculations must use only information actually available at the decision time; do not backfill today's fundamentals into historical results. Revisions append, with their source and timestamp.
6. A level touched is not a fill; a stop is not a guaranteed maximum loss. Daily OHLC cannot resolve intrabar order where multiple levels are touched. Display ambiguity and gaps without inventing execution.
7. Price and volume history must be traceable to source, adjustment status and exchange. Flag unverified corporate-action joins, missing sessions and conflicting sources. Do not mix incompatible price bases silently.
8. Use company-type-specific facts: banks/NBFCs and ETFs cannot inherit inappropriate operating-company rules. A missing or negative ratio requires explicit interpretation. Never infer governance is clean because no issue was found.
9. All claims about management, governance and risks require retrievable dated source passages. AI may summarize evidence but may not create facts, citations or assurances. Human review is required before relying on consequential claims.
10. Version and isolate every run: explicit scanner run, source snapshots, code and configuration. Parallel scan reads source data, writes its own outputs and cannot overwrite live scanner or broker data. Explicitly report partial failures and stale fields.
11. Broker APIs and MCP are read-only in this workflow. No order placement, account changes or external messaging. Use vendor-supported access, respect published quotas and terms, never scrape TradingView. Store credentials securely and exclude them from logs and exports.
12. Any ranking is a user-selected research ordering with disclosed inputs and missing-data behavior; ranking is not a recommendation. Strategy-family overlap is reported, never treated as independent confirmation without validation.
13. Backtests must preserve chronological availability, historical universe and delistings, costs, realistic execution and ambiguity; record all experiments and keep a final holdout untouched. Past outcomes are not evidence of future returns.
14. Distinguish proposed parameters from approved configuration. Until the owner approves, no invented freshness cutoff, minimum turnover, quality threshold, valuation band or position-risk budget is treated as a decision rule.
