### Core Quantitative Logic & Requirements

1. **Zero Macro/Indicator Filters**:
   - Do NOT include any All-Time High (ATH) discount filters.
   - Do NOT include 200-day Simple Moving Average (SMA) proximity filters.
   - Do NOT include Relative Strength Index (RSI) support filters.
   - Strategy S2a is strictly driven by structural price displacement, Fair Value Gap (FVG) creation, liquidity absorption, and candlestick confirmation[cite: 2].

2. **Fair Value Gap (FVG) Formation**:
   - Institutional displacement creates a 3-candle imbalance:
     `Low[bar_i] > High[bar_i - 2]`[cite: 2]
   - The displacement bar (`bar_i - 1`) must show institutional volume:
     `Volume[bar_i - 1] >= 1.50 * SMA(Volume, 20)[bar_i - 1]`[cite: 2]
   - Imbalance height (`Low[bar_i] - High[bar_i - 2]`) must be economically significant:
     `Gap_Height >= 0.015 * High[bar_i - 2]` OR `Gap_Height >= 0.50 * ATR(14)`[cite: 2]

3. **Consequent Encroachment (CE) Retracement & Absorption**:
   - Consequent Encroachment (CE) is the exact 50% midpoint of the imbalance:
     `CE_Level = High[bar_i - 2] + 0.50 * (Low[bar_i] - High[bar_i - 2])`[cite: 2]
   - Pullback must reach the CE zone within a maximum of 15 bars:
     `abs(Low[trigger_bar] - CE_Level) / CE_Level <= 0.02` (within ±2% of CE)[cite: 2]
   - Price must respect the base of the gap; close cannot penetrate below `High[bar_i - 2]` by more than 10%[cite: 2].
   - Volume during the pullback must contract:
     `mean(Volume[pullback_bars]) <= 0.75 * Volume[bar_i - 1]`[cite: 2]

4. **Trigger Candle Gate (At CE Level)**:
   - Evaluated directly on the reaction bar at the CE level[cite: 2].
   - **Strict Doji Rejection**:
     If `(High - Low) == 0` or `abs(Close - Open) / (High - Low) <= 0.10`, the bar is classified as a Doji and rejected immediately.
   - **Bullish Reversal Confirmation**:
     Must be a bullish close (`Close > Open`) and match at least one of the following patterns:
     - **Bullish Marubozu**: `Body / Range >= 0.90` AND `(Close - Open) >= 1.0 * ATR(14)`.
     - **Bullish Hammer**: Lower wick $\ge 2.0\times$ body length AND Upper wick $\le 15\%$ of range.
     - **Bullish Engulfing**: Prior bar bearish (`Close[t-1] < Open[t-1]`), current candle engulfs prior real body (`Open <= Close[t-1]` and `Close >= Open[t-1]`).
     - **Bullish Piercing Line**: Prior bar bearish, current opens $\le \text{Low}[t-1]$ (or Close$[t-1]$), and closes $\ge 50\%$ into prior real body.
     - **Bullish Harami**: Prior bar bearish, current bullish body completely inside prior body (`Open > Close[t-1]` and `Close < Open[t-1]`).
