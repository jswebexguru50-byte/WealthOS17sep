Trading breakouts with gaps- running stocks

Prerequisites and Basics
- Confirm pivot-5 on weekly chart
- Remove penny stocks i.e below ₹50
- Broad markets are bullish (nifty 500/nifty midcap/smallcap above sma/ema 20)
- Consider large-caps /or high mkt-cap midcaps i.e 20K cr

Trend Rule:
- Price above 200 sma and 50 sma
- New week or day starts with gap up (after weekly close)
- New daily / weekly open with gap 2%+ previous week close

Use VPA during contraction
- Volume compression on pullback
- Pullback >= 50% with low volatility

Buy entry
- when previous swing high breaks on daily
- or hourly charts bullish candle on RSI support

Win rate ~ 50% → Risk control is key- sustained buying at different stages is the key

Stop Loss Rule
- Stop 1% below lowest low on daily chart
