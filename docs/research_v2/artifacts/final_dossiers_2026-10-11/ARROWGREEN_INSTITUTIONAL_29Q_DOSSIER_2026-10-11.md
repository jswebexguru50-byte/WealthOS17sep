## Executive assessment

Arrow Greentech’s issuer disclosures identify a specialised green-packaging and anti-counterfeit product portfolio. The issuer’s Q1 FY26 release/presentation reports revenue of ₹416m, EBITDA of ₹142m and PAT of ₹109m, with better sequential margins but materially lower revenue and earnings than both Q4 FY25 and Q1 FY25. The Trendlyne snapshot reports annual CFO ₹43.05 crore, ROCE 27.07%, ROE 20.20%, zero debt/equity and zero promoter pledge; these provider figures remain subject to statutory period/scope reconciliation.

**Evidence state:** **PARTIAL.** Core earnings and product evidence exists, but audited segment, cash-flow, working-capital, governance, customer/supplier, legal and related-party notes remain to be extracted.

**Strengths requiring validation:** specialised product categories; primary-reported Q1 FY26 EBITDA margin of 34.2%; provider-reported positive returns and zero pledge.  
**Risks requiring validation:** Q1 FY26 revenue/earnings decline against both stated comparators; cash-flow and working-capital completeness; customer/supplier and legal/governance disclosure gaps.

## Detailed evidence assessment and research protocol

This section makes the evidence standard visible for every question. It does not turn gaps into negative findings. “Next action” is a limited research instruction tied to a question and its preferred source.

### Q1. Fundamental-only boundary

The approved workstream does not assess price action, volume, supports, technical moving averages or trading liquidity. This absence is deliberate and must not be read as a technical view. No price-series statement appears elsewhere in the dossier. **Evidence state:** NOT_APPLICABLE — Out of scope: fundamental analysis only (deferred); **next action:** none unless the owner changes the analytical mandate.

### Q2. Product utility and operations

A2 identifies three evidence-backed product areas: water-soluble films, bio-compostable films and high-technology anti-counterfeit products. The first two establish a sustainable-packaging orientation; the third establishes an authentication/security application. The current documents do not quantify category mix, identify end markets/geographies, state capacity/utilisation or confirm an order book. The final dossier should avoid translating a product description into a specific customer-use case without a cited management or annual-report statement. **Evidence state:** PARTIAL [A2]; **next action:** extract A1 annual-report MD&A and A3 product/capacity/geography pages, and make an explicit order-book premise check.

### Q3. Competitive advantage

Specialised films and anti-counterfeit products may have technical differentiation, but the current evidence does not prove patents, certification barriers, customer qualification periods, cost leadership or switching costs. A3’s Q1 FY26 56.8% gross margin and 34.2% EBITDA margin are primary financial observations. They improved sequentially versus Q4 FY25, while revenue and EBITDA were lower YoY; neither datapoint proves a sustainable advantage. T1’s ROCE 27.07% and ROE 20.20% require source/period reconciliation. **Evidence state:** PARTIAL [A2, A3, T1]; **next action:** retrieve IP/certification and distribution evidence from A1/A3 and calculate multi-year statutory return/margin trends.

### Q4. Customer concentration

No primary excerpt names a customer, quantifies a top-1/top-5 share or describes contract duration/renewal terms. The report must not treat green-packaging specialisation as diversification. If the annual report provides only a generic credit-risk concentration disclosure, describe it exactly and distinguish it from revenue concentration. **Evidence state:** NOT_DISCLOSED; **next action:** search A1 notes/MD&A and material disclosures; record a dated nil result if the issuer does not disclose customer shares.

### Q5. Supplier, input and pass-through exposure

The gross-margin comparison (56.8% in Q1 FY26, 49.1% in Q4 FY25, 57.5% in Q1 FY25) records outcomes, not causes. **Evidence state:** PARTIAL [A3]; **next action:** extract annual-report purchase/inventory/forex/risk notes and only then assess supplier concentration/pass-through.

### Q6. Market opportunity

## Evidence controls before publication

The final investor-facing version should promote an item from this dossier only after it is connected to a frozen evidence record: source URL or filing identifier, retrieval date, publication/period date, issuer identity, reporting scope, unit and exact excerpt or statement-line reference. Primary statutory facts prevail where there is a conflict with T1. A provider mismatch is retained as a conflict/reconciliation item; it is not silently overwritten and does not become a narrative conclusion.

The Q1 FY26 release is useful because it provides both a management explanation and a comparably formatted P&L table. It remains a single-quarter disclosure. Any statement about recovery, resilience, cash conversion, leverage, demand, pricing or competitive advantage must therefore be tied to the longer statutory series and relevant notes described above. A final drafter should use `NOT_DISCLOSED` when the report and official exchange filings do not disclose an item, and `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` when disclosed source material exists but cannot yet be aligned or calculated. This distinction makes the remaining research workload auditable.

The reviewer should reject a final version if: a provider fact lacks its as-of/period/scope label; an issuer assertion is presented as an independent fact; a calculation mixes standalone and consolidated values; an untraced secondary source supports a numerical claim; or a conclusion claims “no issue” merely because no source has been extracted. It should also check every currency unit: the issuer materials use ₹ million, while T1 values are recorded in ₹ crore.
