## Detailed evidence assessment and research protocol

This section deepens the question-by-question record. It does not add any factual claim beyond the cited source register. “Next action” names the smallest primary-source retrieval or calculation that could resolve the stated gap; it is not a prediction.

### Q1. Fundamental-only boundary

Price direction, chart patterns, volume and technical levels are intentionally excluded under the approved fundamental-only mandate. This is a methodology choice, rather than a statement that price data is unavailable. No subsequent section uses short-term price movement to support a business or valuation conclusion. **Evidence state:** NOT_APPLICABLE; **next action:** none unless the scope is formally extended.

### Q2. What the issuer does

The strongest currently verified description is P2’s statutory standalone segment label, “Telecommunication Products.” It establishes the line of reporting, but does not identify the relative contribution of product categories, services, distribution, manufacturing or consolidated subsidiaries. P3 is the appropriate current issuer index for results presentations, while P1’s MD&A and notes should provide the historical business description, geographic sales and capacity language. The dossier deliberately does not translate a telecom-products label into an assumed handset, EMS, component, distribution or service mix. The order-book sub-question is subject to a premise check: no current primary source in the bundle confirms that such a metric is reported. **Evidence state:** PARTIAL [P2]; **next action:** import P1 MD&A and the latest P3 presentation pages that state products, facilities, capacity and geographic/revenue mix.

### Q3. What could be durable

P4’s statement that higher production volumes and customer wins supported standalone growth is an issuer performance explanation. It may indicate execution momentum, but it does not by itself demonstrate customer switching costs, a cost advantage, proprietary IP, certification barriers or durable distribution strength. T1’s 9.06% ROCE and 8.49% ROE are provider observations; without the invested-capital definition, reported period and audited numerator/denominator, they cannot be used to rank quality or infer persistence. The required evidence standard is specific: a named certification/IP right, documented manufacturing/distribution capability, or a management/annual-report explanation reconciled to results. **Evidence state:** PARTIAL [P4, T1]; **next action:** extract P1 MD&A/BRSR and P3 business-model pages, then calculate aligned statutory returns over multiple years.

### Q4. Revenue concentration

No document in the bundle names a customer or gives a percentage of revenue, receivables or orders. Customer wins are not evidence of low concentration; a new win might reduce or increase concentration depending on its size. The final report must distinguish: named customer disclosure, generic “major customer” disclosure and no disclosure. Contracts, renewal terms and termination rights also need their own source, because a top-customer share alone does not determine renewal risk. **Evidence state:** NOT_DISCLOSED [P1/P3 not yet extracted]; **next action:** search P1 notes, MD&A and material contracts/credit-risk disclosures for customer concentration, then mark a nil result explicitly if none is disclosed.

### Q5. Supplier and input exposure

No current fact identifies a key component, vendor, import ratio, commodity or purchase commitment. It would be inappropriate to infer supplier power from the telecom-products segment. P1 should be searched for inventory valuation, raw-material purchases, foreign-currency risk, credit risk, purchase concentration and any sensitivity note; P3 may describe supply-chain strategy. If management describes pass-through, the final report must state whether it is contractual, historical behaviour or an expectation. Margin behaviour can be analysed only after revenue, material cost and operating cost are aligned by period and scope. **Evidence state:** NOT_DISCLOSED; **next action:** extract the audited cost/inventory/forex notes and test historical gross/EBITDA margins against disclosed drivers.

### Q6. Market opportunity

The current record contains neither an independent market-size study nor an issuer calculation explaining addressable market, penetration or market share. The report will not convert an industry-growth aspiration into an addressable-market fact. A credible market section needs the served product category, geography, time horizon and methodology; company estimates should be labelled as such and separated from independent industry evidence. Capacity headroom needs verified installed capacity and utilisation, not merely future revenue ambition. **Evidence state:** NOT_DISCLOSED; **next action:** take named market statements from P3/P1 and retain the original methodology/source, then seek one primary/industry source only for gaps that affect the conclusion.

### Q7. Reinvestment and cash availability

T1’s negative annual CFO of ₹12.15 crore is notable because the snapshot also contains positive operating/PAT figures, but these values have not been established as the same reporting scope or period. It may reflect working-capital movement, a genuine cash-flow issue, classification or a provider mapping problem. The right test is audited CFO less cash capex, measured across multiple annual periods, with depreciation and project financing as context. Until then no claim can be made about maintenance versus growth capex, internally funded expansion or surplus-cash deployment. **Evidence state:** PARTIAL [T1]; **next action:** extract P1 consolidated cash-flow/PPE notes and reconcile them against P2/iXBRL before calculating FCF and CFO/PAT.

### Q8. Capital allocation

T1 reports return ratios but contains no decision history. High or low ROCE alone does not reveal whether capital was allocated to organic capex, acquisitions, debt reduction, dividends, working capital or idle cash. A meaningful review requires multi-period statutory ROCE/ROIC, reinvestment rate, disclosed capex projects and outcomes of any acquisition/investment. Cost of capital must be a documented assumption rather than an implied hurdle. **Evidence state:** PARTIAL [T1]; **next action:** build a statutory five-year return/cash-use table and extract P1 directors’ report disclosures on investments, acquisitions and distributions.

### Q9. Outlook, catalysts and constraints

The verified current operational explanation is narrow: P4 refers to production volumes and customer wins. It should be presented as management/issuer evidence with date and scope, not converted into a forecast. T1 debt/equity of 0.39x is insufficient to describe financial flexibility without debt, cash, lease and maturity data. A catalyst must be dated and attributable to a filing; a constraint must be linked to an identified capacity, supply, customer, policy, financing or execution disclosure. **Evidence state:** PARTIAL [P4, T1]; **next action:** create a dated catalyst/constraint register from P3 and exchange announcements, with each item tied to a source and measurable milestone.

### Q11. Segment drivers
