## Q2–Q27 evidence map

### Q2. Core product utility

- **Products/segment:** P2 calls the statutory standalone segment “Telecommunication Products.” P1/P3 should establish the consolidated product and service mix, including EMS, distribution and any B2C categories; this brief does not infer mix from the segment label.
- **Customer problem, geography, capacity and utilisation:** not yet extracted. Prioritise MD&A and P3 presentations, then tie each disclosed factory/capacity claim to period and entity.
- **Order book:** no verified order-book disclosure extracted. Treat the order-book premise as unconfirmed, rather than zero.

### Q3. Strengths and competitive advantage

- **Evidence available:** P4 attributes the Q2 FY26 standalone revenue rise to higher production volumes and new customer wins. That is management/issuer evidence of execution, not proof of a durable moat.
- **Return/margin evidence:** T1 reports ROCE 9.06% and ROE 8.49%; margins must be recomputed from period-aligned statutory P&L, not compared with other issuers here.
- **IP, certifications, distribution, switching costs and sustainability:** obtain exact MD&A/presentation excerpts; none is yet verified in this bundle.

### Q4. Customer concentration

No top-customer names, top-1/top-5 shares, contract durations or renewal terms have been extracted. Search P1 notes to accounts/MD&A and material exchange disclosures. No order-book concentration conclusion is permitted.

### Q5. Supplier and vendor concentration

P2 confirms the business classification but does not supply supplier concentration in the extracted statutory header. Import/commodity exposure, single-source components, contract pass-through and cost movement must be taken from P1’s risk/MD&A sections and related notes.
