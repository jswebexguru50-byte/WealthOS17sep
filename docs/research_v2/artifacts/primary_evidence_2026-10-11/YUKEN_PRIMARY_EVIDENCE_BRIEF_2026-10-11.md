# Yuken India Limited — primary-source evidence brief

**Purpose.** Evidence pack for the 29-question fundamental dossier, not an investment recommendation.  Scope is fundamental analysis only; no peer comparison is made here.  `Unknown` means the cited official materials reviewed for this brief do not establish the item.

**Entity / basis.** Yuken India Limited (NSE: YUKEN; ISIN INE384C01016).  Primary reference is the FY2024-25 annual report (year ended 31 March 2025); figures below are consolidated unless stated otherwise and are in ₹ lakh where the report uses that unit. [Y1]

## Q2–Q9: business, market and capital-allocation evidence

### Q2 — product, use case, geography, capacity and order-book premise
- **Products / use case:** the company describes hydraulic power units, valves and solenoids, precision machining, hydraulic cylinders, electric motors, casting for hydraulic equipment and hydraulic pumps.  It positions these in machine-tool, infrastructure and construction applications. [Y1, pp. 14–16]
- **Geography / footprint:** manufacturing units are described in Karnataka, Maharashtra and Haryana, with a national footprint of offices, dealers and service partners. [Y1, pp. 14–16]
- **Capacity / utilisation:** no utilisation percentage or quantified capacity expansion was identified in the reviewed annual-report excerpts. **Unknown.**
- **Order book:** no order-book amount, composition or customer concentration was identified.  The order-book premise is therefore unproven; the final dossier should test revenue concentration from notes/filings rather than substitute an assumed order book. **Unknown.**

### Q3 — moat, durability and erosion risk
- **Company-stated differentiators:** local application-specific hydraulic engineering, R&D/new-product work, customised solutions and national service/dealer coverage. [Y1, pp. 14–16]
- **Durability evidence:** FY25 consolidated revenue grew 7.83% to ₹46,109.38 lakh.  This is a reported historical result, not proof of a durable moat. [Y1, p. 24]
- **Erosion risks:** the company says capital-equipment and auto segments slowed and customers staggered investment; this is relevant to demand cyclicality. [Y1, p. 3]
- **Peer comparison:** intentionally excluded from this standalone brief; requires a separately evidenced Q3 peer pack.

### Q4 — customer concentration / contract renewal
- Named top customers, top-1/top-5 concentration and contract-tenor disclosures were not located in the reviewed sources. **Unknown.**
- Because no order-book disclosure was found, the correct follow-up is audited revenue concentration in customer/credit-risk notes, not a fabricated order-book answer.

### Q5 — supplier and input exposure
- The annual report discloses overseas supplier purchase exposure and foreign-exchange exposure in USD, JPY, GBP and EUR.  At 31 March 2025, consolidated foreign-currency trade payables included USD ₹650.57 lakh, JPY ₹1.71 lakh and EUR ₹92.52 lakh. [Y1, pp. 131–132, 214]
- Single-source suppliers, contractual pass-through terms and commodity-price mechanisms: **Unknown.**

### Q6 — market, penetration and headroom
- Served markets disclosed: machine tools, infrastructure and construction; geographic production footprint is domestic as above. [Y1, pp. 14–16]
- Market size, company market share, methodology and quantified capacity headroom: **Unknown.**

### Q7 — cash flow, capex and funding
- Consolidated FY25 operating cash flow was ₹4,934.16 lakh; purchase of PPE/intangibles including capital advances was ₹5,473.70 lakh.  Cash flow from operations therefore did not cover reported investing outflow in that year before financing flows. [Y1, pp. 158–159]
- Financing flows included ₹892.50 lakh long-term borrowing proceeds, ₹1,015.50 lakh long-term borrowing repayments, ₹1,291.17 lakh net short-term borrowing increase and ₹193.67 lakh dividends paid. [Y1, p. 159]
- Maintenance versus growth capex and project-level return targets: **Unknown.**

### Q8 — returns and reinvestment
- Trendlyne supplementary snapshot in the project’s six-scrip cache reports ROCE 7.76%, ROE 3.87% and debt/equity 0.28 (period/definition must be retained with the raw snapshot before final publication).
- FY25 consolidated PAT was ₹2,460.16 lakh versus ₹1,878.55 lakh in FY24. [Y1, p. 24]
- Incremental ROIC, WACC and acquisition-performance calculations require a controlled calculation pack; **not established** by the sources reviewed.

### Q9 — outlook, catalysts and constraints
- Management’s contextual statement: capital-equipment and auto markets slowed and investment was deferred/staggered. [Y1, p. 3]
- The report contains forward-looking-statement cautions and says actual outcomes may differ materially from expectations. [Y1, p. 2]
- Dated catalysts, formal guidance and a verified bull/base/bear scenario set: **Unknown.**

## Q10–Q17: operating and financial evidence

### Q10 — revenue quality and breadth
- Consolidated FY25 income: ₹46,109.38 lakh; FY24: ₹42,762.71 lakh (+7.83%).  Standalone FY25 income: ₹39,693.03 lakh; FY24: ₹37,097.78 lakh (+7%). [Y1, p. 24]
- The specification’s FY25 XBRL reconciliation benchmark gives discrete consolidated sales quarters of ₹110.20 crore, ₹115.6571 crore, ₹106.8465 crore and ₹124.651 crore, totaling ₹457.3563 crore.  These must be retained as discrete quarters, not replaced by YTD values. [Y2]

### Q11 — segment economics

### Q13 — margins

### Q14 — exceptional and other income

### Q15 — cash conversion
