## Q2–Q27 evidence map

### Q2. Core product utility

- **Products:** A2 identifies Arrow Greentech as a green-packaging-technology company specialising in water-soluble films, bio-compostable films and high-technology anti-counterfeit products. These are the current evidence-backed product categories.
- **Use case:** water-soluble/compostable films address packaging/material applications; anti-counterfeit products address product-authentication needs. A more specific customer/use-case description requires A3/annual-report excerpts.
- **Geography, capacity/utilisation and order book:** not extracted. Do not assume an order book exists; seek A1 annual report/MD&A and A3.

### Q3. Strengths and competitive advantage

- **Documented starting evidence:** A2 describes the company as focused on green packaging technology and the listed specialised-film/anti-counterfeit categories. This supports an innovation/product-positioning lead, not a quantified moat.
- **Operating evidence:** A3 gives Q1 FY26 gross margin 56.8%, EBITDA margin 34.2% and PAT margin 25.1%; versus Q4 FY25, EBITDA margin increased from 28.0% and PAT margin from 19.3%. Versus Q1 FY25, revenue and EBITDA fell, so durability needs more than margin comparison.
- **Provider returns:** T1 shows ROCE 27.07% and ROE 20.20%, supplementary pending statement reconciliation.
- **IP/certifications/switching costs/market share:** extract exact annual-report or patent disclosures; do not extrapolate from product type.

### Q4. Customer concentration

No named customers, top-1/top-5 revenue share, contract duration or renewal disclosure is extracted. Search annual-report notes/MD&A and material disclosures. Order-book premise is unconfirmed.

### Q5. Supplier and vendor concentration

A3 shows Q1 FY26 materials consumed ₹153m, purchases of stock-in-trade ₹114m, inventory change -₹87m and total raw material ₹180m. It does not identify suppliers, import dependence, commodities or contractual pass-through. The Q1 gross margin was 56.8%, versus 49.1% in Q4 FY25 and 57.5% in Q1 FY25; that variation is descriptive, not proof of pass-through.

### Q6. Addressable market

A2 identifies sustainable packaging and anti-counterfeit technology as served areas. No dated TAM, market-share, penetration or utilisation data has been extracted. Obtain source methodology from A3/annual report before reporting industry growth/headroom.

### Q7. Reinvestment runway

- **Provider annual cash signal:** CFO ₹43.05 crore (T1). It supports a cash-flow datapoint but needs reconciliation to audited cash flow and capex.
- **Projects, capacity and funding:** not extracted. Use A1 annual report/presentation. Maintenance versus growth capex and incremental returns are unknown.

### Q8. Capital allocation efficiency

Provider reports ROCE 27.07% and ROE 20.20% (T1). That is not a history, ROIC calculation or WACC spread. Capex, acquisitions, dividends, buybacks and the performance of past investments must be taken from audited statements and annual-report narrative.

### Q9. Outlook and catalysts

- **Primary result context:** A2 says Q1 FY26 revenue was ₹416m, versus ₹573m in Q4 FY25 and ₹661m in Q1 FY25; EBITDA was ₹142m, versus ₹160m and ₹281m, respectively. Management described softer revenue and margin performance amid a changing global backdrop, while noting operational-efficiency gains.
- **Balance sheet:** provider says debt/equity 0.00x (T1). Absolute debt, cash, leases and net-debt/EBITDA require audited reconciliation.
- **Catalysts/constraints:** only state those tied to dated management disclosure after extracting A3/annual-report passages.

### Q10. Revenue growth quality

- **Primary annual/quarter datapoints:** A2/A3 show FY25 revenue ₹2,434m; Q1 FY26 ₹416m; Q4 FY25 ₹573m; Q1 FY25 ₹661m. Thus Q1 FY26 was down 27% QoQ and 37% YoY on the presentation’s reported comparison.
- **Provider annual comparison:** T1 revenue ₹209.04 crore should be reconciled to issuer FY26/FY25 consolidated figures before publication; it is not assumed to be the same period/scope.
- **Organic/inorganic, price/volume/mix, geographic and receivable quality:** not yet extracted.

### Q11. Segment revenue drivers

Current primary evidence identifies product categories, not audited segment revenue/margins. Retrieve segment note and presentation bridge. Do not assert which category led revenue without disclosed segment/product data.

### Q12. Sequential bottoming

A3 provides three adjacent quarter/year reference points: Q1 FY26 revenue/EBITDA/PAT ₹416m/₹142m/₹109m; Q4 FY25 ₹573m/₹160m/₹114m; Q1 FY25 ₹661m/₹281m/₹202m. This is insufficient for an eight-quarter bottoming test and does not establish a bottom. Build the statutory eight-quarter series and working-capital series.

### Q13. Margin trajectory

- **Primary:** Q1 FY26 gross margin 56.8%; EBITDA margin 34.2%; EBIT margin 34.4%; PAT margin 25.1%. A3 shows Q4 FY25 EBITDA/PAT margins 28.0%/19.3% and Q1 FY25 42.4%/30.1%.
- **Interpretation boundary:** the Q1 sequential margin expansion coexists with lower revenue and lower YoY margins; the report should state both, not choose one direction.
- **Drivers:** A2 attributes incremental profitability to operational efficiency, but input-cost/mix/operating-leverage attribution needs audited/management evidence.

### Q14. Quality of earnings

A3 reports Q1 FY26 other income ₹19m, depreciation ₹18m and finance cost ₹1m, alongside PBT ₹143m and PAT ₹109m. It does not establish exceptional-item treatment or a normalised PAT bridge. Extract audited notes before characterising core versus non-core earnings.

### Q15. Cash flow versus profitability

T1 reports annual CFO ₹43.05 crore, while issuer FY25 PAT was ₹630m (A2/A3). These are potentially comparable magnitudes but must first be aligned for consolidated/standalone basis and fiscal period. Capex, receivables, inventory, accruals and FCF are not extracted; no cash-conversion conclusion yet.

### Q16. Capital deployment and utilisation

Provider ROCE is 27.07% (T1). A3’s operating P&L does not identify capex, debt repayment, dividends/buybacks or cash build. Retrieve FY25 cash flow/PPE/debt/equity notes and project disclosures before assessing capital deployment.

### Q17. Relative valuation

### Q21. Audit, quality and board independence

A1 links corporate-governance reports and annual reports; however auditor identity/tenure/fees/qualifications, audit-committee composition, independence/attendance, skills and remuneration have not yet been extracted. Populate with page references from FY25 annual report.

### Q22. Business-model risks

Primary evidence shows revenue contraction in Q1 FY26 vs both Q4 FY25 and Q1 FY25 despite still-high reported margins. Candidate risks for evidence-led review are demand volatility, product/technology competition, raw-material exposure, execution and customer concentration. Leverage appears limited in T1 but needs audited verification. No legal/regulatory/currency conclusion is supported yet.

### Q23. Critical monitoring watchlist

Use a reconciled baseline, then monitor quarterly revenue; gross/EBITDA/PAT margins; CFO/PAT; receivable/inventory movement; capex/capacity utilisation; debt/cash; promoter pledge; and material customer/supplier concentration. Each threshold must be calibrated from verified history, not imposed from the Q1 print.

### Q24. Interest-rate sensitivity

Provider debt/equity 0.00x does not demonstrate nil floating-rate debt. Floating/fixed debt split, borrowing cost, maturities and covenants are not extracted. The 25-bp PAT effect is `NOT_DISCLOSED` until those notes are available.
