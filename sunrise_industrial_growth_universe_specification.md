### CRITERION 3: HIGH TURNOVER & EBITDA GROWTH VELOCITY

#### 3.1 Objective & Rationale
Separates dynamic compounding engines from stagnant value traps. The business must demonstrate explosive top-line revenue velocity and expanding operational profitability driven by fixed-cost leverage.

#### 3.2 Mathematical Velocity Equations

##### 1. Top-Line Turnover (Revenue) CAGR:
$$\text{CAGR}_{\text{Rev}, 3Y} = \left(\frac{\text{Revenue}_t}{\text{Revenue}_{t-3}}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}} - 1 \ge 20.0\%$$
$$\text{YoY}_{\text{Rev}, TTM} = \frac{\text{Revenue}_{TTM} - \text{Revenue}_{TTM-1}}{\text{Revenue}_{TTM-1}} \ge 22.0\%$$

##### 2. EBITDA Growth Velocity:
$$\text{CAGR}_{\text{EBITDA}, 3Y} = \left(\frac{\text{EBITDA}_t}{\text{EBITDA}_{t-3}}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}} - 1 \ge 25.0\%$$

##### 3. Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL):
$$\text{DOL} = \frac{\% \Delta \text{EBITDA}}{\% \Delta \text{Revenue}} = \frac{(\text{EBITDA}_t - \text{EBITDA}_{t-1}) / \text{EBITDA}_{t-1}}{(\text{Revenue}_t - \text{Revenue}_{t-1}) / \text{Revenue}_{t-1}} \ge 1.10\times$$
*Condition:* EBITDA must grow faster than Revenue, proving structural margin expansion.

##### 4. Forensic Cash Conversion Quality Gate:
$$\text{Cash Conversion Ratio} = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^3 \text{CFO}_k}{\sum_{k=1}^3 \text{EBITDA}_k} \ge 0.50$$
*Condition:* 3-year cumulative Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) must equal at least 50% of cumulative EBITDA to disqualify companies generating artificial paper turnover with uncollected receivables. Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) must be stable or decreasing:
$$\text{DSO}_t \le 120 \text{ Days} \quad \text{AND} \quad \text{DSO}_t - \text{DSO}_{t-1} \le 15 \text{ Days}$$

### CRITERION 4: INNOVATION & COMMERCIAL VALIDATION GATE

#### 4.1 Objective & Rationale
Identifies corporate scale-ups, disruptive technology platforms, or R&D-intensive engineering innovators that have passed the proof-of-concept phase, secured customer acceptance, and crossed into scalable, profitable commercial execution.

#### 4.2 Quantitative Validation Metrics

##### 1. Order Book to Revenue Visibility Multiple:
For EPC, defence electronics, railways, and capital equipment manufacturers:
$$\text{OBM} = \frac{\text{Executable Order Book}}{\text{TTM Revenue}} \ge 1.50\times \quad (\text{Target } \ge 2.50\times)$$
*Example:* ₹1,000 Cr TTM Revenue supported by ₹2,500+ Cr confirmed executable order book with delivery schedule within 24–36 months.

##### 2. Innovation & IP Moat Intensity:
Must satisfy at least one of:
- **R&D Intensity:** $\frac{\text{R&D Expense}}{\text{Total Revenue}} \ge 3.0\%$ (applicable to tech/defence/pharma).
- **Proprietary Technology/Patents:** Holds registered patents, proprietary designs, or exclusive technology transfer agreements (e.g., ToT from DRDO or ISRO).
- **Specialized Certification:** Level-3/4 AS9100 aerospace certification, FDA cGMP clearance, or RDSO railway clearance.

##### 3. Profitability Inflection Gate:
- **For New-Age Tech / Turnaround Scale-ups:** Must have crossed operational breakeven, demonstrating positive Net Profit ($\text{PAT} > 0$) for at least **2 consecutive quarters** and positive operating cash flow ($\text{CFO} > 0$).
- **For Established Innovators:** TTM Net Profit growth YoY must exceed 25%:
  $$\frac{\text{PAT}_{TTM} - \text{PAT}_{TTM-1}}{\text{PAT}_{TTM-1}} \ge 25.0\%$$

##### 4. Balance Sheet Solvency & Funding Independence:
$$\text{Net Debt} = \text{Total Debt} - (\text{Cash} + \text{Liquid Investments})$$
$$\frac{\text{Net Debt}}{\text{EBITDA}} \le 1.50\times \quad (\text{Preference for Net Cash Balance Sheet})$$
$$\text{Interest Coverage Ratio} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Finance Costs}} \ge 4.0\times$$

### CRITERION 5: LOW MARKET FLOAT & LIQUIDITY COMPACTION

#### 5.1 Objective & Rationale
Price velocity in equity markets is governed by supply-demand inelasticity. When aggressive institutional buying hits a scrip where 75% to 85% of shares are permanently locked by promoters and long-term domestic/foreign institutions, available public floating supply evaporates, precipitating rapid upward price repricing.

#### 5.2 Ownership Structure & Float Compaction Formulas

$$\text{Total Equity Shares} \equiv S_{\text{Promoter}} + S_{\text{FII}} + S_{\text{DII}} + S_{\text{Strategic}} + S_{\text{Public/Retail}}$$
$$\text{Free Retail Float \%} = \frac{S_{\text{Public/Retail}}}{\text{Total Shares}} \times 100\%$$

#### 5.3 Inviolable Ownership Thresholds

| Ownership Dimension | Minimum Threshold | Target Ideal Range | Disqualification Limit |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Promoter Holding** | $\ge 50.0\%$ | $55.0\% - 74.9\%$ | $< 45.0\%$ |
| **Institutional Stake (FII + DII)** | $\ge 15.0\%$ | $20.0\% - 35.0\%$ | $< 10.0\%$ |
| **Locked Core Base (Promoter + Inst)** | $\ge 70.0\%$ | $75.0\% - 88.0\%$ | $< 65.0\%$ |
| **Free Public / Retail Float** | **$\le 25.0\%$** | **$12.0\% - 22.0\%$** | **$> 35.0\%$** |
| **Promoter Pledge \%** | $\equiv 0.0\%$ | $0.0\%$ | $> 5.0\%$ |

#### 5.4 Institutional Absorption Ratio (IAR)
Measures what proportion of the non-promoter shares are absorbed by smart money institutions:
$$\text{IAR} = \frac{S_{\text{FII}} + S_{\text{DII}}}{\text{Total Shares} - S_{\text{Promoter}}} \ge 0.40 \quad (40\% \text{ of non-promoter float locked in institutional vaults})$$

### CRITERION 6: SMALL-CAP / MID-CAP MULTIPLE RE-RATING ROOM

#### 6.1 Objective & Rationale
Mega-caps (e.g., Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank) face mathematical size saturation; their P/E multiples are fully discovered and priced in. Conversely, emerging Small-Caps and Mid-Caps operate in the valuation discovery window, where market recognition triggers the **Dual Compounder Engine**:
$$\text{Total Shareholder Return} \approx \underbrace{(1 + g_{\text{Earnings}})}_{\text{EPS Growth}} \times \underbrace{(1 + \Delta_{\text{Multiple}})}_{\text{P/E Re-rating}} - 1$$

*Example:* A company growing EPS at 30% whose P/E expands from 20x to 40x delivers a **2.6X return (160% gain)** over 2 years.

#### 6.2 Market Capitalization Guardrails
$$\text{Market Capitalization} \in [₹1,000\text{ Cr}, ₹45,000\text{ Cr}]$$
- **Lower Bound (₹1,000 Cr):** Eliminates illiquid micro-caps, pump-and-dump manipulation risks, and ensures basic institutional investability.
- **Upper Bound (₹45,000 Cr):** Strictly bounds the universe to Nifty SmallCap 250, Nifty MidCap 150, and emerging leaders prior to large-cap multiple compression.

#### 6.3 Re-Rating Headroom Metrics

##### 1. PEG Ratio (Price/Earnings-to-Growth):
$$\text{PEG} = \frac{\text{TTM Trailing P/E}}{\text{3-Year EPS CAGR \%}} \le 1.50 \quad (\text{Optimal } \le 1.00)$$

##### 2. Benchmark Multiple Discount:
The scrip must trade at a meaningful discount to its large-cap group/industry leader:
$$\text{Valuation Discount} = 1 - \frac{\text{P/E}_{\text{scrip}}}{\text{P/E}_{\text{Tier1 Leader}}} \ge 25.0\%$$
*Example:* A small-cap electronics manufacturer trading at 35x P/E while sector benchmark Dixon trades at 85x P/E has substantial headroom for valuation re-rating.

##### 3. Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) Trajectory:
Multiple re-ratings are sustainably catalyzed only when returns on incremental capital exceed the cost of capital:
$$\text{ROCE} \ge 18.0\% \quad \text{AND} \quad \text{ROIC} \ge 15.0\%$$
$$\Delta \text{ROCE}_{3Y} = \text{ROCE}_t - \text{ROCE}_{t-3} \ge 0\text{ bps (Flat or Expanding)}$$
